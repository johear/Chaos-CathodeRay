    ⌕ Command Palette & Flash-to-Location
    6-Point Camera-Projector Calibration
    Click 6+ non-coplanar 3D vertices in the Left 🧊 3D Orbit view (or click ⚡ Auto-Seed 6 Pins), then drag the ①..⑥ Stage crosshairs onto the physical object's real corners. Live ×5 fiducial error whiskers show exact pixel residuals while dragging.
    4-Blade Framing Shutters
    Drag the 4 warm blade bars around the selected surface on Stage: Drag bar inward = Blade %, Drag bar tip = ±45° Tilt swivel, Alt+Drag = Focus Frost, Double-click = Open blade.
    Polygon & Bezier Mask Vertices
    Click ✂ Edit Mask Vertices on Stage to move vertices, click purple edge midpoints to insert points, Double-click any vertex to toggle Smooth Bezier vs Sharp Corner, or Alt+Shift+Click to delete a vertex. Press Esc to return to Surface Geometry.
    Cosmic Cloud Followspot Gestures
    Press ` (Backquote) to steer live on Stage, or drag the ring handles directly. While steering, hold Shift (Stretch), R / Alt (Rotate), M (Morph), S (Shadow), or Scroll (Iris Size). Held modifiers light up live along the bottom ARRI bezel.
    3D Multi-Projector Beam Convergence
    Intersects opposing Left/Right wing projectors on a 3D performer standing in front of the stage screen—eliminating double-shadow parallax and wrapping the body from both angles. Drag the performer live in ◪ Rig view!
    Multi-Projector Optical Engine
    Routes the Canonical Stage across up to 32 projectors with raised-cosine gamma unity (`I₁ᵞ + I₂ᵞ = 1.000`), black-floor uplift, 0–200ms frame-delay sync, or physical 3D Constant-Brightness solving in ◪ Rig view (`5`).
    44Hz LED Pixel Map & USB-DMX / Art-Net
    Samples live WebGL Stage pixels into DMX512 universes over direct browser WebSerial USB (ENTTEC Pro / OpenDMX) or Art-Net UDP 6454 (`node scripts/osc-artnet-bridge.js`). Position fixtures directly on Stage or in ◪ Rig view.
